# Brag Plan: Galaxy Buds Manager for macOS

## What is this app?
A native macOS menu bar app for Galaxy Buds2 Pro battery levels and everyday settings.

## Planning rubric
1. App: Buds2 Pro status and controls, one click from the Mac menu bar.
2. Strongest claim: Left, right, and case battery together, with ANC/Ambient/Off available from the same popover.
3. Visual hook: A tiny menu bar icon opening into a surprisingly complete control panel.
4. Actual UI: The connected Buds2 Pro popover in `screenshots/menu-app-rounded.png`.
5. Shortest satisfying film: 23 seconds.
6. Tone: cinematic/polished; premium, minimal, high motion inside held shots.
7. Audio: Original synth pulse and motion-matched clicks.
8. Caption: Your Buds2 Pro finally feel at home on your Mac.
9. Flow: Mac connects the Buds → click menu bar icon → view three battery readings and select a noise mode.

## The angle
The entire control surface lives behind one tiny icon. Use a premium earbud close-up as a visual hook, then take the viewer through a continuous unfolding of the menu bar and its controls. The animated interaction is illustrative; the menu image is a real capture.

## Hook
An enormous, softly lit earbud macro image and the line “Your Buds. Your Mac.” resolve in the first two seconds.

## Key moments
- The tiny menu bar indicator opens into the real Buds2 Pro popover.
- Left, right, and case percentages separate into giant, crisp numerals, then return to the popover.
- The noise-control selector visibly travels across Off, Ambient, and ANC.

## Outro
App icon, product name, and “One click from your menu bar.”

## User flow worth showing
Connected in macOS → menu bar click → battery status → Buds2 Pro noise selection.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic
- Creative direction: premium minimal product film; stable shots, kinetic content, no poster sequence.
- Interpretation: broad camera moves, precise object motion, short typography, generous settled holds.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 23 seconds

## Visual identity
- Background: #0B111B, inspired by macOS dark surfaces and generated product lighting.
- Accent: #2F8BFF, from the app icon and UI symbols.
- Text: #F5F8FF.
- Display font: local macOS system sans.
- Body font: local macOS system sans.
- Strongest visual element: real Buds2 Pro popover, supplemented by one illustrative earbud studio image.

## Audio direction
- Role: restrained cinematic pulse and sparse tactile accents.
- Music: original synthesized 23-second bed in `composition/assets/music.wav`.
- Treatment: steady low pulse; soft rise at the popover reveal, few click cues, smooth tail.
- Cue guidance: authored pulse at 120 BPM. Major reveals near 3.5s and 12s; sequential battery reveals around 7.0, 7.5, 8.0s.
- SFX posture: sparse, on UI actions only.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Product light — 3.5s
A graphite earbud macro drifts slowly in blue rim light. “Your Buds. Your Mac.” arrives quickly and holds. The product shot is illustrative.
Transition: light line turns into a macOS menu bar.

### Scene 2 — One click — 3.5s
Menu bar icon grows; cursor click sends a small ripple; the real connected popover opens and settles.
Transition: camera follows the battery row.

### Scene 3 — Three batteries — 4.5s
Real popover remains visible. Left 80%, Right 70%, Case 74% resolve as three oversized numerals in sequence, each held to read.
Transition: battery figures contract into the interface.

### Scene 4 — Noise control — 5s
The popover's Off / Ambient / ANC vocabulary becomes a large, interactive segmented control. Selection slides through modes; final state settles on ANC. Label states are illustrative interaction, faithful to the Buds2 Pro app controls.
Transition: control folds into the menu.

### Scene 5 — More within reach — 3.5s
The real popover is prominent. Equalizer, Touch controls, Voice Detect, and Find my earbuds get brief focus, one at a time, without implying feature operation.
Transition: cool blue sweep.

### Scene 6 — End frame — 3s
App icon and name resolve over the premium dark field. “One click from your menu bar.” and project GitHub address hold.

**Audio summary:** quiet sub-bass and glassy tones set scale; soft clicks follow interface action; finale resolves cleanly.
